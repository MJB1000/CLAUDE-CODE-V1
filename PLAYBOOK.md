## How to Use This File

- **At project start:** Strategist reads the Validated sections for proven patterns.
- **After publish:** Strategist adds new patterns as `observed`.
- **At 30-day retro:** Strategist promotes confirmed patterns to `validated` or removes invalidated ones.
- **Quarterly:** Prune patterns that have not been validated after 3+ campaigns. Archive to `PLAYBOOK-ARCHIVE.md`.
- **Cap:** Aim for no more than 30 active patterns. If over 30, prune the weakest `observed` entries.

**Tags:**
- `validated` — confirmed by performance data (metrics from retro)
- `observed` — caught in review or noticed during production, not yet confirmed by data
