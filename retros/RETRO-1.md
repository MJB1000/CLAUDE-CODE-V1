## What Copywriter Got Right First Time

- "No tools, no mechanic, no drama" — perfect Aussie-direct rhythm, locked Round 1
- "Sort your wipers before winter hits — not during" — strong practical-prep close, reused as social ad headline
- Value prop ordering (Fit > Durability > Ease) — correct instinct to lead with biggest differentiator
- "Find Your Wipers" CTA — smart match to site's fit-finder tool
- Three genuinely different ad angles (timing, proof, offer) — not rewording of same line
- Email works for both past buyers and new prospects — no segmentation friction
- "Cheers, The WiperTech Team" — natural Aussie sign-off, matched brand voice

## What the Brief Should Have Included

- **Stats attribution policy** — should have specified upfront whether stats need source lines on cold-traffic pages. Dana had to escalate this.
- **Shipping cut-off time** — operational detail that affects copy. Should be in Available Brand Assets before brief is written.
- **Brand jargon guidance** — "AeroFlex" is a brand term. Brief should flag which brand terms are acceptable for cold audiences vs warm.

## Rules to Carry Forward

- Always add stats attribution on landing pages serving cold traffic from paid ads
- Never use superlative claims ("Most Trusted," "Best," "#1") without comparative proof — use stat-based claims
- Keep brand jargon off cold-traffic pages — use plain language equivalents
- Confirm all operational claims (shipping times, cut-offs) with client before writing
- Put offer mechanics in a dedicated section — don't mix them into benefit copy
- Include brand name in email subject lines (space permitting)
