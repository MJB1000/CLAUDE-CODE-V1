# Campaign Summary — Mornings Subscription Launch

**Prepared by:** Sofia (Strategist)

**Client:** Mornings (DTC specialty coffee)
**Status:** Cleared for launch. Awaiting CD final approval.

---

## Strategy Overview

**Positioning:** Premium, effortless coffee delivery. Not a "subscription box" — a service that makes good coffee the thing you never have to think about again.

**Audience:** Urban professionals 28-40, daily specialty coffee drinkers in AU metro areas. Design-literate, quality-conscious, commitment-averse. They know good coffee — they just keep running out of it.

**Key message:** Your morning coffee, sorted — no commitment, no fuss, first bag free.

**Channels:** Email (launch announcement), Instagram feed ad (1080x1080), Instagram stories ad (1080x1920).

**Budget:** $5,000 AUD total across ads + production.

**Launch offer:** First bag free with subscription signup.

**Core insight:** The audience's objection is not about quality or price — it is about commitment. "Cancel whenever" is as important as "first bag free." Every deliverable leads with freedom, not lock-in.
