## Content Hierarchy (for Dana)

Sections (in priority order):
1. **Hero Image** (most visually prominent): Full-width lifestyle product image placeholder. Sets the mood — warm, minimal, morning light.
2. **Headline** (largest text): "Your Morning, Sorted." — single most important text element.
3. **Subhead** (secondary text): "Good coffee showing up before you've had to think about it. That's the whole pitch." — the witty line, slightly smaller than headline.
4. **Body** (supporting): Three short paragraphs. Even weight. The cancel-anytime paragraph is the objection handler — should feel calm, not defensive.
5. **CTA Button** (high prominence): "Get Your Free Bag" — centered, full-width on mobile, dark fill (#3D2B1F) with light text (#FFFAF5). This is the second most important element after the headline.
6. **Sign-off** (minimal): "Mornings. Coffee that shows up." — brand tagline, small text.
7. **Footer** (standard): Unsubscribe + legal. Smallest text.

Content notes for Dana:
- Headline is the single most important text element — largest, boldest
- Subhead sits directly below headline, smaller but still prominent
- Body paragraphs should have generous line spacing — the short paragraphs are intentional, don't compress them
- CTA appears once, after the body. It is the primary action.
- No social proof section — omitted by design (no testimonials exist)
- Sign-off is a soft brand moment, not a CTA. Keep it understated.
- Image placeholder at top — lifestyle product shot, not a generic stock photo direction
