## Campaign Context

**Performance data:** First campaign for Mornings. No prior data. No MCPs connected.

**CD interpretation:** The word "subscription" triggers commitment anxiety in the target audience. The offer must lead with freedom and quality, not lock-in. The first-bag-free offer is the hook, but the real sell is the experience of not having to think about coffee again.

**What worked (CD direction):** Minimal, confident brand voice. Product quality speaks for itself. No over-explaining.

**What didn't work (avoid):** Subscription jargon ("plan," "commitment," "contract"). Heavy-handed urgency tactics. Overly promotional tone that undermines the premium positioning.

---

## Audience

**Who:** Urban professionals, 28-40. Daily specialty coffee drinkers. They already know good coffee. They buy from local roasters, specialty online brands, or high-end grocery. Income: mid-to-high. Location: primarily Australian metro areas.

### Audience Context

- **What they know:** They understand single origin vs blend. They have opinions about roast profiles. They are NOT beginners.
- **What competitors say:** "Discover new roasters," "Curated for you," "Never run out." Generic convenience messaging. Most subscription brands lead with variety/discovery.
- **What objections they bring:** "I don't want to be locked in." "What if I don't like it?" "I already have a go-to roaster." "Subscriptions are annoying to cancel."

## Objective

Drive subscription signups via the first-bag-free offer. Primary metric: signup conversion rate. Secondary: email click-through rate, ad CTR.

## Key Message

Your morning coffee, sorted — no commitment, no fuss, first bag free. (14 words)

## Tone & Voice

Confident, minimal, slightly witty. Like a barista who knows their craft but doesn't lecture you about it. Specific guidance:
- Short sentences. No filler.
- Wit is dry, not loud. One clever line per piece max.
- Never apologize or hedge. State things directly.
- Conversational but not casual. No slang. No exclamation marks.
- Comparison: think Aesop product copy meets Allpress brand voice.

## Channels

- Email (launch announcement)
- Instagram feed ad (1080x1080)
- Instagram stories ad (1080x1920)

## Constraints

- Budget: $5,000 AUD total (ads + production)
- Launch offer: First bag free with subscription signup
- No superlatives ("best coffee," "finest beans") — unsubstantiated
- No "subscribe" in headlines — reframe as "get" / "start" / "try"
- Email: max 150 words body copy (excluding header/footer)
- Instagram feed ad: max 8 words headline, max 15 words supporting text
- Instagram stories ad: max 6 words headline, max 12 words supporting text
- No customer testimonials (none exist yet) — do not fabricate social proof
- Must include cancel-anytime messaging in email body
- CTA must link to subscription signup page

## Deliverables

### Deliverable 1a — Launch Email

- **Audience:** Existing Mornings customers + new email list signups
- **Objective:** Drive subscription signups via first-bag-free offer
- **Key message:** Your morning coffee is sorted. First bag is free. Cancel anytime.
- **Tone:** Confident, minimal, one witty line allowed
- **Channel:** Email (600px, single column)
- **Constraints:** Max 150 words body. Must include cancel-anytime. No "subscribe" in headline. No fabricated testimonials. CTA links to signup page.
- **Flag:** Do NOT invent customer quotes or social proof. We have none.
- **Flag:** "Subscription" must not appear in the headline or CTA. Reframe around the experience.

### Deliverable 1b — Instagram Feed Ad (1080x1080)

- **Audience:** Lookalike audiences of specialty coffee buyers in AU metro
- **Objective:** Drive traffic to subscription signup page
- **Key message:** First bag free. Your mornings, handled.
- **Tone:** Confident, minimal. Headline does the work.
- **Channel:** Instagram feed (1080x1080)
- **Constraints:** Max 8 words headline, max 15 words supporting text. Product image placeholder (coffee bag). Logo visible. Offer badge for "first bag free."
- **Flag:** Feed ads get 1-2 seconds of attention. Headline must land instantly.

### Deliverable 1c — Instagram Stories Ad (1080x1920)

- **Audience:** Same as 1b
- **Objective:** Drive swipe-up to subscription signup page
- **Key message:** First bag free. Try it.
- **Tone:** Confident, minimal. Even shorter than feed.
- **Channel:** Instagram stories (1080x1920)
- **Constraints:** Max 6 words headline, max 12 words supporting text. Vertical format. Logo top. CTA at bottom (swipe-up). Product image placeholder top half.
- **Flag:** Stories disappear in 15 seconds. Everything must register in 3.
