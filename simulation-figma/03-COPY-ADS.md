## Content Hierarchy (for Dana's wireframe — 1080x1080)

Sections (in priority order):
1. **Logo** (top-left corner): Mornings wordmark, small, white or dark depending on background.
2. **Product Zone** (center, dominant): Coffee bag product shot — this is the visual anchor. Clean, centered, with breathing room.
3. **Headline** (bottom third, overlaid or below product): "Your Morning, Sorted." — largest text on the image. High contrast against background.
4. **Supporting Line** (directly below headline): "First bag free. Cancel whenever." — secondary size, same alignment as headline.
5. **CTA Badge** (bottom-right or bottom-center): Subtle tag or button shape. "Get Your Free Bag" or offer callout.
6. **Offer Badge** (optional, top-right corner): Small tag — "First Bag Free" if headline doesn't carry it alone.

Content notes for Dana:
- Background: use #FFFBF5 (warm off-white) or a muted lifestyle backdrop. NOT a busy photograph.
- Product shot should be clean and centered — studio-style, not lifestyle clutter.
- Text must be readable at mobile feed size (~320px rendered). Test headline at 64px and supporting at 24px.
- Do NOT put all the text on the image. The primary text (119 characters) lives in the Instagram caption, not on the visual.
- Keep the visual minimal. This brand doesn't shout — the image should feel like a calm pause in a noisy feed.
- No lifestyle imagery of people drinking coffee. Product-forward.

# Deliverable 1c — Instagram Stories Ad (1080x1920)

**Copywriter:** Charlie

**Status:** Ready for Review

---

## On-Image Copy

**Headline:** Your Morning, Sorted. (3 words)
**Supporting text:** First bag free. Cancel whenever. (5 words)

## CTA

Swipe Up (platform default) / "Get Your Free Bag" (if custom CTA available)

---

## Content Hierarchy (for Dana's wireframe — 1080x1920)

Sections (in priority order):
1. **Logo** (top center, safe zone): Mornings wordmark, small. Below the Stories UI elements (clock, profile pic).
2. **Visual Zone** (top 50-60%): Product shot of coffee bag — large, centered, clean background. This is the dominant visual.
3. **Text Zone** (bottom 30-40%): Headline + supporting text. Left-aligned or centered.
   - "Your Morning, Sorted." — 72px Bold, #1A1A1A or white depending on contrast.
   - "First bag free. Cancel whenever." — 28px Regular, same color, below headline.
4. **CTA Zone** (bottom 15%): Swipe-up area. Keep clear for the swipe-up affordance. If custom CTA button is allowed: "Get Your Free Bag" centered above the swipe zone.

Content notes for Dana:
- Stories ads are consumed in 5-15 seconds. The entire message must register in a single glance.
- Top 10% and bottom 20% of the frame are partially obscured by Stories UI. Keep critical text in the middle 70%.
- Background: solid #FFFBF5 or a very subtle gradient. No busy imagery behind text.
- The vertical format = product shot gets more real estate. Use it. Make the bag feel premium and close-up.
- Do NOT add more than 2 lines of text. This is a glance medium.
- "Cancel whenever." used for consistency across all three deliverables. Clear, unambiguous no-commitment messaging.
