## Verdict
- **26% GPAM is achievable — but not on the current trajectory.** The single lever is **MER**. Hold it
  near 25% and you clear 26% in Sep–Dec; let it drift to 30% and you miss every month (still profitable
  on volume, but ~$83k less H2 net profit).
- **July is a locked loss** (~−$35k) — the post-EOFY hangover; already baked in.
- **Sale months are the structural risk.** Aug (Father's Day) sits *at* the line even with discipline,
  because discounts lift VCR. BFCM/Christmas clear it only if MER holds ~26%.
- **Caveat:** revenue is held constant across scenarios to isolate the margin lever. Cutting spend too
  hard can cut revenue — the goal is **efficiency (better ROAS), not just less spend.** This is exactly
  the daily call the Scorekeeper surfaces and the Head of Growth makes.

## What this means for the hub
The Scorekeeper's #1 daily job is the **MER watch** — because at these volumes, MER is what decides
whether the year lands at 21% or 26%+ GPAM. Wiring Meta (live MER) is therefore the highest-value
Phase-2 item.
