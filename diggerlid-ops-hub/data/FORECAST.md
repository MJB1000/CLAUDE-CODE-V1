# H2 2026 forecast — extrapolated from current data

**Revenue basis:** Shopify `net_sales` (after discounts/returns, before GST), AUD. Jan–Jun actual;
**July completed from mid-month run-rate** ($176k net through 15 days → ~$365k full month);
Aug–Dec = **calendar-driven scenario** (Aug: Zip Mat launch + Father's Day; Nov: BFCM; Dec: Christmas).
**These Aug–Dec revenue figures are scenario assumptions, not predictions.**

**Margin model (EE baseline):** GPAM% = (1 − VCR) − MER. VCR 0.46 BAU / 0.48 sale months.
Fixed costs $74,831/mo. Net profit = GPAM$ − fixed. **GPAM target = 26%.**

## Two scenarios — the whole story is MER discipline

| Mon | Rev (net) | **Current trajectory** (spend creeps ~30% MER) | | **Target discipline** (MER ~25%) | |
|---|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| | | GPAM% | Net profit | GPAM% | Net profit |
| Jul | $365k | 11% ⚠ | −$35k **loss** | 11% ⚠ | −$35k **loss** |
| Aug | $420k | 20% ⚠ | $9k | 25% ⚠ | $30k |
| Sep | $340k | 24% ⚠ | $7k | **29%** ✓ | $24k |
| Oct | $330k | 24% ⚠ | $4k | **29%** ✓ | $21k |
| Nov | $580k | 24% ⚠ | $64k | **26%** ✓ | $76k |
| Dec | $430k | 22% ⚠ | $20k | **26%** ✓ | $37k |
| **H2** | **$2.46M** | **21% blended** | **$70k** | **24.4% blended** | **$153k** |

Full-year 2026 net revenue ≈ **$4.8M** either way (revenue is volume-driven; the scenarios differ on
**margin**, not top line).

## Verdict
- **26% GPAM is achievable — but not on the current trajectory.** The single lever is **MER**. Hold it
  near 25% and you clear 26% in Sep–Dec; let it drift to 30% and you miss every month (still profitable
  on volume, but ~$83k less H2 net profit).
- **July is a locked loss** (~−$35k) — the post-EOFY hangover; already baked in.
- **Sale months are the structural risk.** Aug (Father's Day) sits *at* the line even with discipline,
  because discounts lift VCR. BFCM/Christmas clear it only if MER holds ~26%.
- **Caveat:** revenue is held constant across scenarios to isolate the margin lever. Cutting spend too
  hard can cut revenue — the goal is **efficiency (better ROAS), not just less spend.** This is exactly
  the daily call the Scorekeeper surfaces and the Head of Growth makes.

## What this means for the hub
The Scorekeeper's #1 daily job is the **MER watch** — because at these volumes, MER is what decides
whether the year lands at 21% or 26%+ GPAM. Wiring Meta (live MER) is therefore the highest-value
Phase-2 item.
