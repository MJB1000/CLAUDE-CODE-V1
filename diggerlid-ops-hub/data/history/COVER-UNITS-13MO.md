# Cover units, 13 months — the "cover decline" investigation

Question: "cover units down 33% — when did the drop start?"
Answer: **there is no structural drop.** Covers are flat around a stable baseline;
the −33% (Jul25→Jul26 PCP) is a base-effect artefact — Jul 2025 was a peak, Jul 2026 is normal.

## The real AOV driver: dilution by new cheap volume
| Line | Jul 25 | Jul 26 |
|---|--:|--:|
| Hero enclosures | 213 | 129 (flat) |
| PRO Mat ($219) | 0 | 407 |
| Quick Release Coupler ($29) | 179 | 976 |

~1,100 sub-$220 units/month that didn't exist a year ago. AOV fell because the mix
diluted, not because covers left. Source: Shopify net_items_sold TIMESERIES month, pulled 2 Aug 2026.
