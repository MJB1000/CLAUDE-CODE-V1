## What the history establishes

1. **Profit has always been concentrated in two months** — EOFY (Jun) and BFCM (Nov) carry the year. The other
   eleven months hover around break-even. This is a structural pattern, not a 2026 problem.
2. **Costs have genuinely improved.** VCR fell from ~53% (2025) to ~48% (2026) and CPA from $132 → $96 PCP.
   The operations side is working.
3. **AOV is the regression.** $395.67 → $251.50 PCP (−36%), below the 13m average of $312.86 in every month
   since May 26. This is the single metric that has moved consistently against the business.
4. **July 2026 is the worst MER in the window** (41.4% / 2.42x) against a 13m average of 29.6% / 3.38x.
5. **Plan targets are conservative against history.** The Aug/Sep plans assume AOV $300 (13m avg $312.86)
   and CVR 1.37–1.6% (13m avg 2.39%). Both sit *below* trailing performance.

## Caveats
- 2025 model has **no data for Feb/Mar/Apr 25**; Jan 25 present. Only Jul–Dec 25 are used in the 13m window.
- 2025 records **Shipping Cost = $0** in every month — freight is bundled inside Product Cost (52.1% of ex-GST
  revenue in Jul 25). 2026 splits it out (34.21% product + $24/order shipping). **VCR is comparable; the
  product/shipping split is not.**
- Ad spend is `Total Advertising` (Meta + $500/mo fixed advertising). 2026 is Meta-only + live API.
