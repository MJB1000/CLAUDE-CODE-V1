Cost / signup (left axis, $) Signup→conversion rate (right axis, %) ▨ hatched / hollow = partial & immature week

What it says

  • Conversion is flat (~29%), cost/signup is not. Signup quality barely moves week to week — nearly 1 in 3 email signups eventually buys — while cost/signup swings from $69 to $278.
  • Sale periods wreck signup efficiency. The EOFY weeks (15–28 Jun) hit ~$275/signup — spend rose 55% while signups halved — yet those signups converted highest (33–35%). Fewer, pricier, higher-intent.
  • Cheapest list growth = BAU prospecting weeks (~$70–90). Late May / early June bought signups at a third of the EOFY cost and still converted ~29%.
  • The lever is cost/signup, not conversion. To lower blended cost-per-converting-signup, protect top-of-funnel signup capture during sales — don't starve the popup when budget shifts to retargeting.
