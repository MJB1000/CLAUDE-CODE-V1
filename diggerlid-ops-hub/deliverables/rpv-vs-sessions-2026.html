RPV = net sales ÷ sessions. The inverse relationship means revenue scales sub-linearly with sessions — each extra 1,000 weekly sessions has added ~$0.15 less value per visitor, i.e. diminishing revenue on incremental (mostly paid) traffic. Part of the link is mechanical (RPV = net ÷ sessions), but a flat RPV would mean revenue scaling 1:1 with traffic; the downward slope is the diminishing-returns signal.
