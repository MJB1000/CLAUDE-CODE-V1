Email Popup — 365-Day Review

Submission rate & cost per signup · Aug 2025 – Aug 2026 · Alia popup (launched Oct 2025)
The popup was most efficient Jan–Apr 2026 (3%+ submit, ~$70/signup). Scaling paid traffic ~3× from May halved the submit rate (3.8% → 1.0%) and doubled cost/signup ($52 → $141). More traffic ≠ more signups — the extra paid visitor barely engages the popup.

What to do

  • Judge signup efficiency by submit rate, not volume. May–Jul added traffic but the popup captured it at <1.2% — the marginal paid visitor is a poor signup prospect.
  • Sale periods are the worst capture windows. Every traffic surge (EOFY, July) tanked submit rate — check the popup isn't suppressed during sales, and it recurs at Father's Day.
  • Protect the Jan–Apr playbook (3%+ submit, ~$70). That mix — leaner, higher-intent traffic — is your cheapest list growth.
