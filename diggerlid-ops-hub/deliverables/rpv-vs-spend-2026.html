RPV = net sales ÷ sessions. Dot colour = MER (spend ÷ net). As monthly Meta spend rises past ~$120k, RPV falls and MER climbs toward 30–44% — diminishing returns on incremental spend in BAU months. June is the exception: $198k spend stayed efficient (MER 25%) because EOFY-sale demand absorbed it. The lever isn’t a hard spend cap — it’s spending into demand and lifting conversion; the same $ buys far less in a no-promo month (see July: $162k → RPV $3.15, MER 44%).
