DiggerLid · Reach and Media Report

Twice the reach, cheaper per person

Last 12 months (23 Sep 2025 to 22 Sep 2026) against the 12 months before. Meta media, video, grease sales and email list. All figures AUD. Verified rows were pulled on 23 Sep 2026 from Meta insights, Shopify and Klaviyo; reach and video rows come from a same-day Ads Manager pull that matches the verified spend to the dollar.

Media spend rose 86%. People reached rose 125%, video plays 171% and full video completions 259%. CPM fell 5%, cost per click fell 32% and the email list added 3.4 times as many profiles as the year before.

Sources and caveats
  • Windows: Meta and Shopify 23 Sep 2024 to 22 Sep 2025 versus 23 Sep 2025 to 22 Sep 2026. Email: calendar months October to September, with September 2026 to the 22nd.
  • Verified in this workspace on 23 Sep 2026: spend, impressions, clicks, attributed carts and purchases (Meta insights via the diggerlid-mer endpoint); grease sales and orders (ShopifyQL); new profiles and popup signups (Klaviyo).
  • Marked "Ads Manager": reach, frequency and video rows from a direct Ads Manager pull the same day (DiggerLid AU account, which also delivers NZ). Its spend and impressions match the verified rows exactly. The live endpoint does not request reach or video fields; a reach endpoint is written and ready to deploy so these refresh automatically next time.
  • Meta-attributed purchases and value overlap with email, organic and direct. Read them as efficiency trends, not revenue. Shopify net sales are ex GST.
  • Spend and ROAS figures are for internal use. For an external partner deck, lead with reach, plays, completions, growth rates, countries and the email list.
