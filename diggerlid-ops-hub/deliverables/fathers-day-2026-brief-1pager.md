## G — Goal

Drive gift-buyers and self-buyers to Pro Mat Plus and multi-mat bundles, lifting basket size while protecting margin in a discount month.

| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| AOV | ≥ $315 |
| Effective discount | ≤ 20% (15% on mats) |

## A — Audience

- **Primary:** Gift-buyers — partners and family of tradies/operators, buying "something he'll actually use."
- **Secondary:** Self-buyers and existing customers — operators, mechanics, 4WD/hunting dads (zero-CPA list carries the discount).
- **Avoid:** Generic clearance framing; cold paid spend behind deep discount; putting spend behind loss-making lines (grease couplers).

## Core Mechanic (non-negotiable)

1. **15% off mats** (Pro Mat Plus hero) — the headline.
2. **2 for $499 + free shipping** — flagship multi-buy (already live; stays the anchor).
3. **Tradie Dad Packs** — bundles bake in the mat discount, ladder AOV: Site Sorter ~$309 → Two-Machine Pack ~$549 → Full Rig ~$599 → The Depot ~$879.
4. **GWP** — free Cap/Keyring (shown at RRP) over ~$300; low COGS, margin-safe.
5. **Post-FD flash** — "Didn't get what you wanted": 20% self-buy, 48h, list-only.

**Rule:** Value lives in the bundle, not the discount depth. Pro-Mat-led only. The list carries the discount; never stack the 15% on the 2-for-$499 pack (Option A).
