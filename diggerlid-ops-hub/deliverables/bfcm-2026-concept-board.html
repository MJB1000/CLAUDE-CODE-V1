Black Friday · Cyber Monday 2026

BFCM CONCEPT BOARD

Five campaign territories for evaluation. Context: Nov forecast $605k ex-GST · MER ≤26% sale target · offers = % + GWP + bundles (never flat $) · ~34% of Pro Mat Plus buyers are women (gift buyers).

#1

🎨 TAKE COVER

Paintball · Under Attack

Big idea. Black Friday as a full paintball ambush. Deals fly like paint — get behind something or wear it. The only safe machine on site is a covered one.

Through-line. Paint splatter = every hazard gear cops, made visible and fun. The demo IS the proof: splatter an uncovered digger next to a covered one — pull the cover, spotless.

TAKE COVER — YOU'RE UNDER ATTACK
INCOMING: DEALS FROM EVERY DIRECTION
GET HIT OR GET COVERED
DIRECT HITS ON PRICES. ZERO HITS ON YOUR GEAR.

Visual system. Yellow splats on black only — one accent used violently. Goggle-POV frames. Ambush cadence: Scouts spotted → Under fire → Direct hit (live) → Ceasefire (last hours).

⚠ July paintball shoots (calendar Pt 1 & 2) slot straight in. Keep it playful-tradie, not military.

#2

🇦🇺 ALL AUSSIE ADVENTURES

Outback mockumentary

Big idea. A loving parody of the iconic outback-bumbler format: our own khaki-clad 'expert' tours Australia wrecking everything except the covered gear.

Through-line. Every segment ends in disaster around the machine while the cover shrugs it off. Whatever he does to it — it's covered.

ALL AUSSIE ADVENTURES IN MACHINERY PROTECTION
“Now, a lot of blokes will tell ya a tarp'll do. Those blokes are wrong.”
BIG COUNTRY. BIGGER SAVINGS.
Ep 3: THE PILBARA INCIDENT

Visual system. Mockumentary video-first; episode title cards; red-dirt texture; our own recurring character (e.g. 'Gravel Grant').

⚠ Parody the archetype/format only — never the show name, actor likeness, scenes or logo in paid media.

#3

🧱 BRICK BY BRICK

Big Kids. Bigger Toys.

Big idea. Diggers are grown men's toy diggers at 1:1 scale — and every kid knows the rule: look after your toys and pack them away when you're done.

Through-line. Toy care = machine care = covers. Lands doubly at Christmas (toy season + the ~34% female gift-buyer segment).

BIG KIDS. BIGGER TOYS.
PACK YOUR TOYS AWAY PROPERLY
SOME ASSEMBLY REQUIRED. PROTECTION INCLUDED.
THE TOYS GREW UP. SO DID THE TOY BOX.

Visual system. Brick-built excavators on black, stud/pixel motifs — brick yellow ≈ brand yellow. Bundles framed as 'sets'.

⚠ STRICT IP line: generic brick-built aesthetic only — never the LEGO name, logo or minifigures.

#4

🏏 COVERS ON!

Aussie summer cricket

Big idea. Clouds roll in at the G and the cry goes up — COVERS ON! The ground staff sprint; the pitch gets protected. Same rule on your site.

Through-line. The only sporting moment on earth whose hero is literally a protective cover. And Nov 2026 is an Ashes summer in Australia.

COVERS ON!
RAIN'S COMING. DEALS ARE HERE.
THE GROUND STAFF DON'T MUCK AROUND. NEITHER SHOULD YOU.
BAD LIGHT NEVER STOPPED A SALE

Visual system. Ground-staff sprint energy with machines; black scoreboard graphics, yellow lettering; sale phases as session times (First Session · Tea · Final Session).

⚠ Generic cricket only (no team/broadcaster marks). AU-only resonance — fine.

#5

🍺 KNOCK-OFF DEALS

Tools down. Prices down.

Big idea. The sacred double meaning: knock-off = the best moment of a tradie's day; knockoff = the cheap imitation you'd never put on your machine. Genuine gear at prices that look like knockoffs — dropping at knock-off time.

Through-line. THE MECHANIC: a new deal drops at 3:30 PM sharp, every day of the sale. Daily return traffic, an SMS moment, a countdown that means something.

KNOCK-OFF DEALS. GENUINE GEAR.
TOOLS DOWN. PRICES DOWN.
NEW DEAL EVERY DAY AT KNOCK-OFF (3:30 SHARP)
DON'T BUY A KNOCKOFF. BUY AT KNOCK-OFF.

Visual system. 3:30 wall clock yellow-on-black; esky lids, utes at the gate; recurring KNOCK-OFF 3:30 stamp badge; gag: shredded tarp 'KNOCKOFF' vs DiggerLid cover 'KNOCK-OFF PRICE'.

⚠ Needs 7 days of 3:30 operational discipline. 'Knockoff' must always resolve to genuine in the same breath. Dec closer: LAST KNOCK-OFF OF THE YEAR — PARK IT PROTECTED.
