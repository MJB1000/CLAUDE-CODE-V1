Bottom line. The blog skill works: the test article scored 0 fails. But the site itself is telling Google the wrong address for all 49 product and collection pages, and the file AI engines read describes DiggerLid as "lids for excavator buckets". Fix those two before any article goes live, or the article will link into pages Google is told to ignore.

1. Site blockers

These are on the live site, not in the blog. Each one weakens every page, including new articles.

Critical

49 product and collection pages point their canonical at a 404

dev · theme

Every product (23) and collection (26) page tells search engines its real address is /a/shop/products/<handle>. That URL returns 404 for browsers and Googlebot alike. The cause is a block in layout/theme.liquid tagged dl-bc-v2, which fires when the DeepLumen app's ap_deployed metafield is true. The same block exists for blog articles, so any article the app touches would inherit it.

Fix: remove the dl-bc-v2 block, or set ap_deployed to false, so Shopify's own {{ canonical_url }} applies. Find out if the DeepLumen app is still meant to be live. Then request reindexing of the PRO Enclosure, PRO Mat and KAJO pages in Search Console.

Critical

llms.txt describes the wrong business and links to dead pages

dev · app

/llms.txt says DiggerLid makes "precision-fit lids designed to safeguard excavator buckets". It links to /a/shop/collections/... URLs that 404. Google ignores llms.txt, but other AI engines and shopping agents read it, and right now it teaches them the wrong product.

Fix: turn off whichever app generates it (likely the same DeepLumen app), or replace it with a short accurate file: machinery covers, Pro Enclosure, PRO Mat, KAJO grease, with real /collections/ links.

Critical

The blog is empty and shows template placeholders

you · theme editor

/blogs/news has 0 articles. Its "You may also like" section renders "Example collection" placeholders, and the article template hides the author.

Fix: in the theme editor, turn on Show author for blog posts and hide "You may also like" until 3 or more articles exist. Rename the blog from "News" to something searchable, such as "Guides" or "The Dig". The handle can stay news.

High

Homepage has two H1s and a generic title

dev · theme

The H1s are "PRO MAT" and "DIGGERSHIELD UP". The title is "Digger lid excavator covers – DiggerLid" (39 characters). The April audit flagged both, and both are still live.

Fix: one descriptive H1, for example "Mini Excavator Covers, Enclosures and Work Gear". Demote the banner labels to H2. Use a title like "Mini Excavator Covers & Enclosures | DiggerLid".

High

Test and junk pages are in the sitemap

you · admin

Google is being sent: /collections/pro-enclosure-luke-pdp-au-copy ("Luke PDP INT"), /pages/faq-test, /pages/size-guideold, /pages/https-www-diggerlid-com-au, /pages/https-www-diggerlid-com-au-products-digger-lid-1-7, and two empty search-results pages.

Fix: unpublish them, or hide them with a seo.hidden metafield, and 301 any that once had links.

High

44 pages have no meta description

you · admin

That's 28 of 30 collections and 16 pages, including the size guide and the gift and PRO Mat Plus landing pages. Another 21 run over 160 characters and get cut off. All product pages do have one.

Fix: write 130 to 150 characters each, starting with the money collections: enclosures, covers, grease, PRO Mat.

High

The size guide has no H1, no meta, and no crawlable links pointing at it

you · admin

"Will it fit my machine?" is the number one question. The page that answers it (541 words) gets no inbound links in the crawled HTML. It is probably linked by script only.

Fix: add an H1 and a meta description, and plain links to it from the Pro Enclosure page, the enclosures collection and every enclosure article. It is the best internal-link target on the site.

High

Product schema has no reviews, shipping or returns

dev · theme or review app

The Product JSON-LD has name, offers and sku, but no aggregateRating. Yet the PDPs show 4.7 from 246 reviews and 4.9 from 139. Shipping and return policy details are also missing, and the homepage has no Organization or WebSite schema.

Fix: turn on the review app's schema output, and add shippingDetails, hasMerchantReturnPolicy and Organization with sameAs links to the socials.

High

About page is 147 words

you · content

E-E-A-T stands for experience, expertise, authority and trust, and it starts with who is behind the brand. The founders' real story is the strongest asset: landscaping tradies, 2018, the wet seat and the mattress bag. The site barely tells it, and articles have no author page to link to.

Fix: rewrite About to 600 words or more, with photos of Joel and Luke, then link every article's author box to it.

2. Blog skill upgrades

These answer your questions directly. All seven are within reach this week.

Did we clone or extract everything we needed?

No. The first pass distilled 9 of claude-seo's 25 sub-skills into rules. The second pass found four more things worth taking:

  • three stdlib checkers that run here (content quality, claim verification, templated-meta detection)
  • a humanizer for plain text
  • the FLOW framework's dual-surface content scorecard
  • its pre-write audit checks

The rest needs paid API keys (DataForSEO, Google Ads, GCP NLP) or doesn't apply. 38 of the 41 FLOW prompts turned out to be the same boilerplate, so nothing is lost there.

Upgrade 1: vendor the four stdlib scripts, MIT, with the license kept, and run them as a post-draft gate.

Should we run a mini audit each time?

Yes. A new site_inventory.py is built and was used for this report. It crawls the sitemap in about 90 seconds and outputs every page's title, meta, H1s, H2s, word count, links in and out, and missing alt text.

Upgrade 2: make it step 0 of the skill. Re-crawl if the inventory is over 7 days old. Before writing, check four things:

  1. Cannibalisation against every existing title and H2.
  2. Link targets for the article.
  3. Whether the product pages the article links to are healthy (canonical, meta, H1).
  4. Template defects that would undercut the article.

How do we keep the blog in brand tone of voice? Should we read all pages?

Read all pages once, not every time. All 88 pages (about 61,000 words) are now a corpus. A tone-of-voice guide is being built from the best on-brand pages, your proven ad rules and real customer language. It includes measured style stats, preferred words, inconsistencies across the site, and a 30-point scoring rubric.

Upgrade 3: every draft self-scores against the rubric and must hit 24/30, with before/after rewrites as calibration. Refresh the guide every quarter by re-running the crawl.

How do we handle interlinking?

Both directions, every time. Most sites only link out from a new article. The bigger win is linking into it.

Upgrade 4: the skill picks 5 to 10 outbound targets from the inventory, at most one link per target and no repeated anchors. Then it outputs an inbound link list: 3 to 5 existing pages (product pages, collections, the size guide, older articles) with the exact sentence and anchor to add. An article with fewer than 3 inbound links is treated as not launched.

Upgrade 5: keep a content-map.md registry. Each article's primary keyword, cluster, pillar and links go there, so the hub-and-spoke structure stays intact and no two articles chase one keyword.

How do we decide the keyword focus?

Score the shortlist, don't guess. Upgrade 6 replaces judgment with a 100-point score per candidate:

FactorWeightHow it's scored
Source fit25Does the video or post actually answer it? Below 15 means the keyword is disqualified, whatever its volume.
Intent fit20Share of top-10 results that are articles. Under 40% is a critical mismatch, so the product page owns it.
Business value20How directly it leads to a product: grease and enclosure terms score high, generic tips low.
Demand15Planner volume where sourced. Autocomplete presence and People Also Ask depth otherwise.
Winnability15Weak competitor pages, forums or thin content in the top 5 score high. Note the planner's "competition" measures ad bidders, not organic difficulty.
Cluster gap5Fills an empty spoke in the content map.

The test article's choice, "how to load a grease gun", would score about 80. It beat "screw in grease cartridge" because product pages own that search result.

What else would lift results most?

Upgrade 7: connect Google Search Console. It's the one data source that would change keyword decisions most. claude-seo's quick-win rule is position 4 to 10 with more than 50 impressions: queries you already nearly rank for, which an article can push onto page one. Today the skill has real volume data only for grease.

4. What this audit couldn't see

  • Rankings, clicks and impressions: no Search Console access.
  • Core Web Vitals: the PageSpeed API quota was used up today, so this is pending.
  • JavaScript-rendered content: the crawl read server HTML only. Links added by scripts are not counted, which is why some inbound counts read 0.
  • Backlinks: not checked.
