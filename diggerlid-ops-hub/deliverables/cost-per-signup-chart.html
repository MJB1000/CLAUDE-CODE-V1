This is blended — total marketing spend ÷ all new subscribers — so it rises when spend grows faster than capture. It doubled ($38 → $69) in step with the wider efficiency decline. Because every ALIA profile carries the Meta ad_id, the real prize is cost-per-signup by campaign — which broad/grease ads fill the list cheaply even when they sell poorly. That's the next cut.
