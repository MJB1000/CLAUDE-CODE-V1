The real trend
Over 25 months, list-capture efficiency improved dramatically — cost per signup roughly halved after a step-change in Oct 2025 (signups jumped from ~540 to ~1,900/mo). The short 7-month view looked like decay; the long view is a big win that has recently plateaued.
