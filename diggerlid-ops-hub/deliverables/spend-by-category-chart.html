The read
Spend chased volume, not efficiency: Grease ran the best ROAS all year (5.4 → 2.4) on a shrinking budget share, while Portable got the July cash injection at its worst-ever ROAS (1.44). The budget is mapped upside-down to both efficiency and retention.

Cart → purchase rate

Purchases ÷ add-to-carts, by category. Read with care: Meta's add-to-cart pixel under-fired through H1, so ratios above the 100% funnel ceiling are a tracking gap, not reality. Trust the H2 trend.

The read (H2, reliable window)
Once tracking stabilised, cart→purchase converged to ~26–29% across all three categories (Jul: Portable 27% · Grease 26% · Covers 29%). So cart-close is not where the categories differ — the efficiency gap is upstream, in click quality, CPA and AOV, not in how well each product closes from cart.

Outbound clicks

Clicks that leave Meta for the site, by category — a proxy for demand/interest the ads generate.

The read
Covers pull the most click interest all year (and a big July spike to ~51k) — yet convert at only mid ROAS (1.90). That's a merchandising/AOV opportunity, not a demand problem. Portable's July click surge (~27k) landed right as its ROAS collapsed — we paid for clicks that didn't convert.
