**Campaign Period:** 26 Aug – 4 Sep 2026 (10-day sale) · Pre-sale launch sent 9 Aug · Post-Father's-Day flash 7–8 Sep
**Duration:** 10-day sale + 48-hour post-event flash
**Campaign Type:** Seasonal Sale (gifting)

## Headline System

**Hero (desire):** The Father's Day gift he'll actually use.
**Subhead (function):** 15% off mats · 2 for $499 + free shipping · order by 2–3 Sep for delivery · sale ends 4 Sep.

Rule: Headline = emotion (the useful gift). Subhead = mechanics (offer, cutoff, end date). Always both.

---

## Core Mechanic (non-negotiable)

1. **15% off mats** — Pro Mat Plus is the hero and the headline. A shallower cut than 20% protects GPAM while still giving a real reason to buy.
2. **2 for $499 + free shipping** — the flagship multi-buy (2 × Pro Mat Plus, mix any colours; ~$99 saving). Already live from the 9 Aug launch; it stays the anchor offer through the campaign.
3. **Tradie Dad Packs** — bundles bake in the mat discount and ladder AOV rather than discounting deeper.
4. **GWP (healthily priced)** — free branded Cap or Keyring, shown at its RRP, on orders over ~$300. Low COGS and a high-value threshold keep it margin-safe while making the gift feel generous.
5. **Post-Father's-Day flash** — "Didn't get what you wanted": a 20% self-buy offer, 48 hours, list-only (zero CPA), launching the day after Father's Day.

**Rule:** This is a gifting and product campaign at ≤20% effective discount. Value lives in the bundle, not the depth. Pro-Mat-led only — the highest-AOV product is the only one that clears CPA at a discount. The list carries the discounted volume; never put cold paid spend behind a deep discount, and never stack the 15% on the 2-for-$499 pack.

### Offer structure (Option A — confirmed direction)

The standing 2-for-$499 remains the flagship multi-buy for the whole period. The 26 Aug sale layers 15% off single mats and the accessory bundles around it. No stacking on the 2-pack. This keeps one consistent multi-buy in market; the sale widens the range and adds urgency plus the post-event flash.

### Bundles — Tradie Dad Packs

Indicative pricing on confirmed mat prices (Pro Mat Plus $299, Original $219, 2-for-$499). Accessory prices to confirm before final pricing.

| Bundle | Indicative price | Contents | Role |
|--------|------------------|----------|------|
| The Site Sorter | ~$309 | Pro Mat Plus + Magnetic Tool Mat + Boom Bottle Opener | Entry gift |
| The Two-Machine Pack | ~$549 | 2 × Pro Mat Plus (the 2-for-$499 core) + Digger Wipes + GWP | Hero — push this |
| The Full Rig | ~$599 | Pro Mat Plus + a Cover + Magnetic Tool Mat + Digger Wipes | Premium |
| The Depot | ~$879 | 2 × Pro Mat Plus + Cover + Magnetic Tool Mat + Digger Wipes + Bottle Opener + GWP | Big-ticket / whole-yard |

Every pack heroes Pro Mat Plus and never sells it alone, fixing the sold-alone AOV dilution. The ladder ($309 to $879) sits well above a solo mat, so the sale lifts basket size instead of eroding it.

## Customer Service & Logistics

**What CS needs to know**
- Sale runs 26 Aug – 4 Sep; post-FD flash 7–8 Sep (self-buy, 20%).
- 15% off mats; 2-for-$499 includes free shipping; GWP auto-applies over ~$300; the 15% does not stack on the 2-for-$499 pack.
- Shipping cutoffs for Father's Day delivery: QLD, SA, NT, WA, TAS order by Tue 2 Sep; VIC, NSW, ACT by Wed 3 Sep. After a state's cutoff, recommend the digital gift card.

**Key FAQs**
- When does the sale end? → Friday 4 Sep (two days before Father's Day).
- Will it arrive in time? → If ordered by your state cutoff (2 or 3 Sep). After that, gift card delivers instantly.
- Can I mix colours on the 2-pack? → Yes, any colour combination.
- Is the free gift automatic? → Yes, on orders over ~$300.
- Missed the cutoff? → Digital gift card arrives instantly and covers the offer.

**Operational notes**
- Confirm stock cover for a 10-day sale across Pro Mat Plus and every bundle component (magnet mat, wipes, keyring/cap, covers, bottle opener).
- Set up bundle SKUs with auto-applied discount and GWP threshold logic.
- Confirm carrier lead times against the advertised cutoffs (assumed calendar days to Father's Day; business-day math may pull cutoffs one day earlier).
- Surface the digital gift card as the last-minute fallback.
