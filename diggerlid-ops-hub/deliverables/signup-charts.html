2 · Signups vs signup→conversion rate

Weekly, May–Aug 2026. Conversion = Shopify Alia customers with ≥1 lifetime order.
Signups (left) Signup→conversion % (right)

Conversion holds ~29% no matter the signup volume — even the low-volume EOFY weeks (Jun 15–22) converted highest (33–35%). ~1 in 3 signups buys.

Signups = Klaviyo profiles $source="Alia sign-up". Signup rate = signups ÷ Shopify sessions (visitor→signup proxy, not true popup view→submit). Conversion = Shopify tag:Alia customers with ≥1 lifetime order ÷ all Alia customers created that week (denominator differs ~10% from Klaviyo; lifetime-to-date, so the last week is immature).
