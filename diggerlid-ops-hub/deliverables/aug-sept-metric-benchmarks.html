The margin equation
GPAM% = (1 − VCR) − CPA ÷ AOV. Only AOV and CPA move the percentage — CVR grows the dollars. That's why the plan leans on basket size and media efficiency.

Why the ~20–30% store-wide returning rate looks higher than cohort repeat
Shopify's monthly returning rate pools every customer across ~4–5 years; a cohort's 12-month repeat is one batch in one window. Rule of thumb: returning rate ≈ 1 − (1 ÷ lifetime orders per customer) — ~1.3 orders/customer lands you at ~26%. It's carried by grease reorders, so don't use it to justify cover/mat CPA.
