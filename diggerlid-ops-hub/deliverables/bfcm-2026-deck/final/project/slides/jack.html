Meet Jack Clacker: the HOST

Back​story

1 · The country town

Offscreen. He reckons AI has stolen all the local jobs.

2 · The call

The boys need help with Black Friday. Off to the big smoke.

3 · Ready to teach

Every demonstration reveals a better way. He hesitates, then adopts it.

4 · The lesson

Tough, and still look after your machine, gear and body.

5 · Heading home

A ute of bargains. 'Good trip. Got the boys sorted.'

The old way

On the tools forty years, by his own count. From a long line of hard men, all 'retired at forty'.

Wrong wisdom

Hands out old-man country know-how with total authority, and it's wrong every time.

Respects the boys

'These guys know their excavators, grease and on-site protection.' He just assumes they need his wisdom too.

The lament

Is there still a place for an outback bushman in this new world of AI?

Physical comedy

Clumsy, falls over constantly. Never notices, never reacts, never winks.

Inflated contribution

Takes credit for every win. Luke closes the deal; Jack: 'Sometimes you've just got to be firm.'

Bad knees, bad back

He's got bad knees and a bad back. But doesn't everyone?

Quietly coming around

Hesitant to adopt their methods, but we can see he knows they work. His behaviour changes; his commentary doesn't.

Positive, never cruel

On the team's side. The joke is his sense of his own importance, never the boys or the customer.

Lovable at heart

Warm, engaging, always trying. All he wants is to share what he knows.

Catch​phrases

'G'day, I'm Jack Clacker, and this is my backyard.'

Opens every cut.

'Never needed one.'

Said about every product, just before it proves him wrong.

'Old trick.'

The lead-in to bush wisdom that turns out to be wrong.

'Good trip. Got the boys sorted.'

'Been saying that for years.'
