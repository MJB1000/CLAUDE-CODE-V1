Mid-sale from Mon 23 Nov sells to a new market: gift buyers. The hero is a three-brand collab pack, Titan Sox × DiggerLid × 3DPro: one PRO Mat, Titan Sox socks and two 3DPro Buckets in the same pack, at one bundle price ([$X] to set). It sells on its own gifting landing page with a separate offer and discounts, not the sale page. Lessons carried over: the Father's Day gift page converted 0.5% because it was generic with no product depth; do not send gift traffic to the sale page, which leads with grease; make the shipping cut-off explicit. To set: pack price, sock pairs, page offers, stock and fulfilment, revenue split, partner promotion.
