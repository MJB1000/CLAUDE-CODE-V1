DiggerLid's Black Friday Adventure: Jack Clacker heads down from the country to help the boys get the word out about their sale. He respects their expertise: 'These guys know their excavators, grease and on-site protection, but I imagine they called me in for some real outback wisdom.' He arrives ready to teach, but his demonstrations keep revealing better ways of doing things. He is hesitant to adopt their methods, although we can see he knows they work. By the end he has learned the brand's central lesson: you can be tough and still look after your machine, your gear and your body. He heads home with a ute full of Black Friday bargains, ready to teach his mates, and still reckons he taught the boys a thing or two. This replaces the older EOFY framing: Jack is now positive towards the team, and the comedy comes from his inflated sense of his own contribution.
