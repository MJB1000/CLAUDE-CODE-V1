# BAU Copy Patterns

These are the evergreen, non-sale patterns from DiggerLid's Meta account. The sources are:

- the Meta Ad Library teardown of 122 active ads, sorted by impressions (26 Apr 2026)
- evergreen ROAS benchmarks
- the locked structure from the owner's corrections

Measured top-30 stats (ROAS-ranked, sale windows excluded) live in `bau-top-30.md` once the `/api/ads` pull has run. **Where that file and this one disagree, that file wins.**

## What the evergreen winners have in common

These points are observed across the top 25 by impressions.

- **Two pillars carry the account.**
  - Covers and enclosures account for 11 of the top 25. KAJO grease accounts for 8.
  - PRO Mat appears only once in the top 25, so it is under-indexed.
- **Video dominates.**
  - 18 of the top 25 are video. The copy has to work under a talking head or B-roll, so the hook must work on mute in the first 2 seconds.
  - Captions carry the hook, not music.
- **Shipping appeared in 23 of 25** (as free shipping at the time).
  - Owner rule from 7 Oct 2026: say **"Fast shipping Australia wide"** instead. Don't use free-shipping claims or thresholds.
  - 2 Year Warranty appears in 14 of 25. Afterpay, PayPal or Zip appears in 6, on high-ticket items only.
- **No discounts.**
  - BAU ads contain no "$X off", "BOGO" or flash framing.
  - The promotional weight sits in shipping, warranty and proof.
- **Long life.**
  - The median top-25 ad had run 205 days.
  - The Milwaukee-adapter static ran 605+ days, and the DiggerShield static 543+.
  - Lesson: evergreen copy must not date itself. Avoid seasons, "this week" and specific prices unless the ad will be refreshed.
- **Personalised variants win.**
  - Machine-model variants (Kobelco, Yanmar, Wacker Neuson, Bobcat, Toyota Huski).
  - Tool-brand variants ("Your Milwaukee deserves better", "Your Dewalt gun deserves better").

## Emoji system

### 1. Opener emoji (one, at the start of the hook)

| Concept | Emoji |
|---|---|
| Stop doing X | 🛑 |
| Rain, weather, washout | 🌧️ |
| Gravel as villain | 🪨 |
| Knees, body, years on the tools | 🧎 (never 👴. Age framing risks Meta's personal-attributes policy) |
| Grease, KAJO | 🛢️ |
| 4WD, overlanding | 🚗 |
| Camping, outdoors | 🏕️ |
| Gift buyer, partner | 👩 or 🎁 |
| Founder-built | 🛠️ |
| Towing, trailers | 🚛 |
| FIFO, travel | ✈️ |
| Money, cost maths | 💰 |
| New product, launch | 🚨 (BAU launch only, not sale) |

Add 🤩 at the end of the opener line on evergreen product ads.

### 2. Feature checklist (locked)

| Product | Set |
|---|---|
| PRO Mat and Plus | 💪 🛠️ 🧎 🪗 ✅ |
| Pro Enclosure and KAJO | 💪 🛠️ ⚡ 🪣 ✅ |
| Everything else | ✅ ticks for each line, or ✔️. Keep to one style per ad. |

### 3. CTA

Use `Shop Now ⬇` or `👉 Shop now at diggerlid.com`.

### Density

| Tier | Emoji |
|---|---|
| Short | 1 to 2 |
| Medium | 6 to 9, mostly the checklist |
| Long | 6 to 10 |

In story paragraphs, no emoji. The opener line (opener emoji plus 🤩) and the checklist are the only emoji zones. The CTA ⬇ doesn't count toward tier limits.

## The BAU archetypes

Pick one per primary text. The three default texts should use three different archetypes or hooks.

1. **Problem-frame opener.** Name the villain, then reveal the product. Ran as BJP at 4.52x.
   - "Still kneeling on soggy cardboard?"
   - "The old grease gun system? Outdated."
2. **Benefit stack (POV).** Short hook, then a six-line emoji stack. Ran as POV at 4.26x.
3. **Personalisation.**
   - Tool brand: "Your Milwaukee deserves better." Ran as Milwaukee_ASMR at 7.55x.
   - Machine model: "Running a Kobelco SK17?"
4. **Customer story.** A real named customer, their order history, what it proves. Get permission. Use real numbers only.
5. **Old way vs new way.** 1966 spring plunger against the KAJO screw top. Close: "Old way, new way. Choose one."
6. **Cost maths (rain maths).**
   - About 250 working days a year.
   - Rain over 5mm falls on about 55 days a year (BoM capitals average ⚠️ confirm before locking).
   - About 30 of those wash you out properly. Save 20% of them and that's 6 days.
   - At a $1,000 to $1,200 day rate, that's $6,000 to $7,200 a year, against a $699 enclosure.
   - Close: "Pays for itself the first day it saves you."
7. **Founder POV.** "We built this because we were sick of…" The founders are real landscaping tradies.
8. **Objection handler.** "Will it fit my machine?" Answer it in two steps.
9. **Checklist or non-negotiables.** "Four non-negotiables for a proper work mat. Cardboard doesn't tick any of them."
10. **Persona shift.** The same product reframed for 4WD, the tradie wife, a farmer, a truckie or FIFO.
11. **Educational / Trojan horse.** "How to make your grease gun BETTER." A tip-led opener, with the product as the answer.
12. **Social proof lead.** Open on a verbatim review quote ("If you have a digger, you need one of these."), then the product.
13. **UGC creator.** Paid talent to camera. Quote their on-camera lines in the copy (they're scripted, so quoting is fine), but never present paid talent as a verified customer review.
