# Voice, Audiences and Rules

## The voice

A plainspoken Aussie operator talking to one mate on site. The founders were landscaping tradies. Write like you've been on the tools, because the brand has.

**Do:**

- Use full stops over commas. "Quick. Easy. Back on the job."
- Use concrete numbers. "490 GSM." "10mm padding." "Under 20 seconds." "200+ machines."
- Use Aussie idiom, lightly:
  - sorted
  - this arvo
  - smoko
  - stuffed
  - bloody tough
  - rack off
  - wet bum
  - DigHeads
- Dunk on the problem, never the customer. Cardboard is stupid. The bloke using it is just doing what he's always done.
- Let the punchline land and walk away. Don't explain the joke.
- Use one in-joke per ad, at most.

**Don't:**

- Use em dashes. Not once, in any field.
- Use corporate words:
  - elevate
  - unlock
  - revolutionary
  - game-changing
  - seamless
  - solution
  - ultimate
  - cutting-edge
  - premium-grade
  - innovative

  The Shopify PDP copy uses some of these. **Don't** copy it straight into ads.
- Pad with empathy, e.g. "We know how frustrating it can be..."
- Stack more than one exclamation mark.
- Write ALL CAPS sentences. One CAPS word for punch is fine ("Grease EASY").
- Use "we're diggin' it". It's banned.

## Audiences

Write to one audience per primary text.

| Audience | Who | What they want | Voice notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Owner-operator earthmover** (default) | Male, 28 to 50, runs a 1 to 5 person civil, landscape or excavation outfit, owns 1 to 3 minis on finance | Billable days, a protected asset, looking pro on site | Money and uptime. "Keep working, keep earning." |
| **Maintenance-minded operator** | Greases their own gear: operators, farmers, mobile mechanics | Fast, clean, no wasted grease | Time saved, mess avoided, tool-brand pride |
| **Site mechanic** | Works under machines | Their body, their parts, their time | Knees, back, lost sockets |
| **Farmer** | Multi-machine, remote | Durability, value, fast delivery to the back of beyond | Slower and warmer. Golden-hour, practical. |
| **Truckie, tower** | Tows daily, caravanners, boaties | A clean hitch, no corrosion | "One less thing to clean." |
| **FIFO** | Swing rosters | Luggage that survives the trip | Tough-gear credibility. Don't joke about the job. |
| **4WD, camping** | Weekend recovery and camping | Somewhere dry to kneel or lie | Adventure, under the rig, swag, mud |
| **Gift buyer (partner)** | 30 to 60, buying for a tradie partner | A useful gift he'll actually use, plus the bragging rights | Warm, conspiratorial, knowing. Never mock him. |

## Profanity and Meta policy

- Never write "f**k" in any spelling. It gets ads rejected.
  - Swap in **stuffed**, **bent** or **rooted**.
  - "Shitty", "bastard" and "rawdogging" have run fine in body copy.
  - Keep them out of headlines.
- **Personal attributes policy.** Meta rejects copy that asserts or implies the reader's age, health, physical condition, finances or other personal traits.
  - OK: "20 years on the tools? Your knees know it."
  - Risky: "You're too old for this." / "Got bad knees?"
  - Talk about the situation or a third person, not a diagnosis of "you".
- Use before-and-after framing carefully. Show the job changing, not the body.

## Claims and ACCC

- Only make claims the catalogue or the live site supports. Everything else is marked `[CHECK]`.
- "Australia's first modular work mat" is approved. "World's first" needs proof.
- Reflective strips "help you be seen". They never "keep you safe".
- "Waterproof" is fine for the PVC fabric. Don't call a whole enclosure "sealed" or "airtight". The top front is open by design.
- "Made in Australia" applies to DiggerShield only. Everything else is "Australian owned".
- Tax angles, e.g. "tax-deductible for your business": never say "guaranteed". Mention them only if relevant.

## Never trash your own products

- When selling the PRO Mat Plus, never knock the OG.
- When selling the enclosure, never knock the Quicky or the original covers.
- Frame upgrades as "you asked, we built".
