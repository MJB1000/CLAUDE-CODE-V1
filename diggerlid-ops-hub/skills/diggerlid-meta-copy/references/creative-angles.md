# Creative Angles

Use this file when the brief needs fresh hooks. Generate widely, then cut hard. Ship only the hooks that pass the "would a tradie screenshot this and send it to his mate?" test.

## Twelve lenses for any product

Run the product through at least five lenses, then pick the best three hooks.

1. **The villain, personified.** "Cardboard had a good run. 40 years. Time to retire it."
2. **The number.** Lead with the most surprising true number. "490 GSM." "200+ machines." "Under 20 seconds."
3. **The moment.** The exact second the pain hits. "That first sit on a wet seat at 6:45am."
4. **The maths.** Turn an annoyance into dollars a year.
5. **Old vs new.** Name the year or the old way. "1966 called. It wants its grease gun back."
6. **The mate test.** Peer status. "Your mates will ask where you got it. Tell them, or don't."
7. **The confession.** Founder voice. "We built the first one out of a mattress bag. It was rubbish. This isn't."
8. **The checklist.** "Does your [X] pass the test?"
9. **The micro-segment.** Name the machine, tool brand or trade. "Kobelco SK17 owners, this one's for you."
10. **The overheard line.** A real review as the hook. "'If only there were an option for 6 stars.'"
11. **The reversal.** Flip the expectation. "The best thing about this mat? You'll forget you own it. Your knees won't."
12. **The other user.** The product in an unexpected place, e.g. a PRO Mat under the 4WD, a Draw Bar Cover on the horse float.

## Hook formulas that suit the voice

| Formula | Example |
|---|---|
| "Still [doing the bad thing]?" | "Still kneeling on soggy cardboard?" |
| "[Villain] can get stuffed." | "Gravel can get stuffed." |
| "Your [tool brand] deserves better." | "Your Makita deserves better." |
| "[Thing] is OUT. [Product] is IN." | "Cardboard is OUT. PRO Mat is IN." |
| "When others [give up], you [keep going]." | "When others pack up, you keep working." |
| "[N] [things] for a proper [category]." | "Four non-negotiables for a proper work mat." |
| "Right. Let's do some [X] math." | "Right. Let's do some rain math. Earthmover edition." |
| "You asked. We built." | Launch or upgrade framing |
| "Stop [verb]-ing [villain]." | "Stop fighting spring plungers." |
| "If you [own X], you need [Y]." | Lifted from a review |

## Freshness rules

- One new hook in each batch of three must be something the account hasn't run. Check it against the hook bank in `bau-copy-patterns.md`.
- Seasonal BAU (winter rain, harvest, Christmas holidays on the 4WD) is allowed. Keep it out of ads planned to run more than 60 days.
- Creativity lives in the hook and the close. The checklist stays locked and boring on purpose. It's what converts.
