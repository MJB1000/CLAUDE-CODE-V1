# Watchlist — oversight register (so nothing slips)

The closed-world rule: **every open loop lives in exactly one register, or it doesn't exist.**
Four registers — Scorecard (metrics), Projects Board (work), **this Watchlist** (risks / external
threads / hygiene), Decision Log (choices). The Chief of Staff files anything that surfaces here,
with an owner and a next-check date, in the same session it appears.

## Register format
| Item | Category | Owner | Severity | Last touch | Next check | Age | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| *(example)* Zip mat stock cover for Aug 5 launch | inventory | Marshal | high | — | T-14 | — | open |

## Watch areas (duties bolted onto existing seats — no new headcount)
| # | Watch | Owner | Trigger |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Funnel & site health** | Scorekeeper | checkout-completion or add-to-cart drops >20% vs 30-day norm; PDP/variant error patterns (e.g. the "9-tube grease minimum" bug) |
| 2 | **Inventory vs plan** | Marshal | launch item with <6 weeks cover at forecast velocity |
| 3 | **Promo hygiene / offer drift** | Marshal | any active discount code or sale asset >7 days past campaign end (GWP comms, floating codes, "swap back" tasks) |
| 4 | **External commitments** | Chief of Staff | any partner/creator/supplier thread silent >14 days (creators contacted, EWH contract, grease supplier TBD) |
| 5 | **Platform & compliance risk** | Chief of Staff | ad-account warnings; policy rejections spike; giveaway launched without T&Cs logged; shipping/GST policy changes |
| 6 | **Data integrity** | Scorekeeper | sessions:orders ratio breaks pattern; pixel event volume drops; two sources diverge >15% |
| 7 | **Cost creep** | Chief of Staff | any new recurring cost; any line flagged idle 2 months (e.g. "global shopify (idle) $76/mo") |

## Escalation matrix
- **Now (ping the Head of Growth):** ad-account risk · site/checkout down · launch T-1 no-go ·
  GPAM$ pacing below fixed costs.
- **Monday brief:** everything else breaching.
- **Monthly retro:** cost creep · aged watchlist · register health.

*Not staffed yet:* people/capacity (leave, rain-dependent shoots) — the Marshal includes a capacity
line in T-7 readiness rather than anyone "monitoring" people.
