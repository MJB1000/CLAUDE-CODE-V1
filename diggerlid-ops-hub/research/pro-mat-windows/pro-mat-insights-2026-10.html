PRO Mat: how long people take to buy, and who buys Plus

12 Oct 2026 · Shopify orders & sessions
23 Mar to 12 Oct 2026 · 2,249 paid PRO Mat orders

The short version: PRO Mat is a fast decision. Three in four buyers purchase the same day they first arrive, about 2 visits over about 4 days on average. Plus buyers decide the same way as Original buyers, but they come more from Meta, live more in Queensland, buy earlier in the day and spend about $160 more per order.

1. Conversion window

Days from first visit to purchase (all PRO Mat orders)

Orders with tracking data (n = 2,164). A second cluster at 15 to 28 days (195 orders) is larger than days 8 to 14: the "think about it, come back" group.

Share bought by each checkpoint: Original vs Plus

Plus buyers take about half a day longer on average (4.3 vs 3.8 days). 90% have bought by day 15 for Plus and day 13 for Original.

Number of visits before buying

Buyers who come back more than once (45%) average 3.7 visits over about a week. Original and Plus are near identical (2.2 visits each).

Where orders come from (order referrer)

Plus leans on Meta creative (43% social). Original sells more on reputation, with 39% arriving direct.

Time of day orders are placed

One in five Plus orders lands between 5 and 9am, nearly double Original's 11%.

Orders by state

Plus over-indexes in Queensland. Orange and Camo are about one third QLD. Other includes TAS, ACT, NT and NZ (NZ about 2 to 4%).

Plus orders by colour

Pink has the biggest basket ($552 per order), likely gift-led. Grey is evenly spread across states.

What to do

  • Fix the choice on the product page. Add a clear Original vs Plus comparison ("Plus = zips + reflective + 4 colours") and default the selector to the bestseller. The page now converts at half its old rate.
  • Attribution and retargeting. Keep 7-day click (about 80% of Meta buyers). Run 28-day retargeting with a fresh proof creative at days 10 to 14 to catch the 15 to 28 day cluster.
  • Sell Plus as a system. 32% already buy 2+ mats, so lead Plus ads with "zip two together" and test a 2-mat bundle.
  • Targeting. Test Queensland-weighted Orange and Camo creative, and early-morning (5 to 9am) delivery for Plus. Use gift framing for Pink.
  • Past customers. Cross-sell PRO Mat to customers 3 to 18 months after their first order, not in the first 30 days.
