  • The inland belt leads. Darling Downs (Dalby, Roma, Chinchilla, Wallumbilla), Emerald and Mackay and Isaac buy 3 to 5 times the state average.
  • Industrial coast is the volume. Mackay 29, Bundaberg 24, Gracemere 19, Gladstone 17 and Rockhampton 12.
  • Acreage fringe of SEQ. Fernvale 17, Jimboomba 10, Gympie 21: lifestyle blocks and machines, and Camo's strongest pocket.
  • Coastal cities lag. The Gold Coast (4 per 100k), Brisbane (6) and the Sunshine Coast (8) are 70% of Queenslanders but a small share of mats.
