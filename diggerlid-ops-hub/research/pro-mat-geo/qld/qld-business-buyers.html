Who buys PRO Mats in QLD

About 28% of Queensland PRO Mat orders come from businesses (178 of 639 since March). Diesel workshops, civil and plant hire, and farming lead, and business orders carry more mats.

What to do with it

  • Multi-mat bundle (3 or 5 mats) with "kit out every service ute" framing. Business buyers already buy 6 to 13 at a time.
  • Meta audiences in regional QLD: diesel fitters, heavy-vehicle mechanics, plant hire and earthmoving owners, graziers.
  • Keep the consumer checkout. Half of business buyers use personal email, so trade accounts aren't needed. Add a "Business name (for your tax invoice)" field so more businesses identify themselves.
