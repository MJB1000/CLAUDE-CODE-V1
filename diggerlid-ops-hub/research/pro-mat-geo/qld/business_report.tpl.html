Who buys PRO Mats in QLD

Regional Queenslanders buy far more mats per person than city people, and about 1 in 4 orders comes from a business. This report combines where mats sell, weighted by population, with who is buying.

The short version

  1. Country beats city. Per person, the Darling Downs buys about 12 times more mats than Brisbane.
  2. About 1 in 4 orders is a business. That's 173 of 639 orders, and it's a minimum.
  3. The businesses fix machines, move dirt or farm. Mechanics, civil and plant hire, and farmers make up half of them.
  4. Businesses buy more at once: about 2 mats per order, against 1 for everyone else.
  5. In the city, a third of buyers are businesses. In the country, a quarter are. Out there, farmers and operators buy on their personal email.

What to do

  • Spend Meta budget on regional QLD (Darling Downs, the SEQ acreage fringe, Central Highlands, Mackay, Gympie). Volume per person is highest there.
  • Add a 3 or 5 mat bundle with "kit out every service ute" framing, aimed at workshops and plant hire businesses in Brisbane, Ipswich and Mackay.
  • Target audiences of diesel fitters, heavy-vehicle mechanics, earthmoving and plant hire owners, and graziers.
  • Add a checkout field labelled "Business name (for your tax invoice)", so more business buyers show up.

Original vs Plus, and which colours

Plus has taken over in Queensland, and Orange leads the colours. The colour mix changes by area and by buyer type.

The short version

  1. 4 in 5 mats sold in QLD are now Plus. That's a bit more than the rest of Australia (78% vs 74%).
  2. Orange is the favourite. Almost 1 in 3 QLD mats is Orange.
  3. Plus brings in the money. Each Plus sells for about $60 more, so Plus is 83% of PRO Mat sales dollars in QLD.
  4. City buyers love Orange (43%). Country buyers spread out more across Original, Grey and Camo.
  5. Businesses lean Grey (1 in 4). Personal buyers lean Camo (1 in 5).

Against Australia, QLD buys more Orange (31% vs 28%) and Camo (18% vs 15%), and less Original. Businesses pick Grey far more than personal buyers (25% vs 17%). Personal buyers pick Camo (21% vs 11%).

What to do with colour

  • Lead city and coast ads with Orange. It's 43% of the city mix.
  • Lead business and workshop ads with Grey (the fleet or bundle angle), especially around Mackay.
  • Show Camo and Original in southern inland and acreage ads (Darling Downs, Toowoomba, the SEQ fringe).
  • Treat Pink as a niche (10%). Test a gift angle, and don't stock to the Townsville spike.
