# PRO Mat geo breakdown (12 Oct 2026)

**Units.** PRO Mat units sold (net items) by shipping state, 23 Mar to 12 Oct 2026. Source: ShopifyQL. 2,870 AU units in total.

**Population.**
- States: ABS ERP at 31 Mar 2026.
- Capitals: ABS Greater Capital City ERP at 30 Jun 2025.

**Index.** Index = share of units ÷ share of population × 100, so 100 = selling in line with population.

**Metro vs regional.** These are classified by capital-city postcode ranges, which is an approximation. Small cells under about 20 units are noisy.

**Files.**
- `geo_results.json` holds every figure.
- `geo_calc.py` reproduces them.
