Standouts
  • Regional is the market. Two thirds of mats ship outside the capitals. Sydney (3.2 per 100k) and Melbourne (4.5) barely buy, and Perth is the best capital (9.1).
  • Queensland is Plus country. Only 20% Original. Orange is 32% (45% in Brisbane) and Camo is 18%, the highest of any big state.
  • Grey Plus wins NSW (27%) and regional WA (32%). Orange is weakest in WA (23%).
  • Original holds in SA (32%), WA (30%) and Sydney (33%). These markets need the Original vs Plus comparison most.
  • Pink is regional (10% regional vs 7% capitals), strongest in regional NSW and QLD. It's weak in SA (5%).
  • Meta: weight spend to regional postcodes in QLD, WA, SA and NT. Lead QLD with Orange and Camo, NSW and WA with Grey, and test gift-framed Pink in regional NSW and QLD.
