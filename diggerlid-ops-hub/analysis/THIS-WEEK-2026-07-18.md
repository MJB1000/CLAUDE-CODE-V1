# This Week's Diagnosis — 12–18 Jul 2026
*Custom to what actually happened in the last 7 days, not the general plan.*

## The one problem this week: the grease-coupler acquisition trap
Order **volume** rose (up to 73/day) while **AOV cratered** ($332 on 13 Jul → $183–190 by 15–18 Jul). The product mix says exactly why:

| Product (last 7d) | Orders | Net | Avg/order |
|---|--:|--:|--:|
| Quick Release Grease Coupler | **226** | $6,589 | **~$29** |
| KAJO Grease Packs | 116 | $31,326 | ~$270 |
| Battery Grease Gun Adapter | 81 | $5,753 | ~$71 |
| Package Protection (attach) | 205 | $1,875 | ~$9 |
| PRO Mat | 94 | $21,239 | ~$226 |
| Pro Excavator Enclosure | 21 | $11,631 | ~$554 |
| Covers (1.7T + Quicky + Drawbar) | ~56 | ~$10,050 | ~$180 |

**Grease-ecosystem order-lines (~443) outnumber protection order-lines (~79) roughly 5:1 this week.** The single biggest order-driver is a **$29 grease coupler at 226 orders**. That's the AOV crash — and, given "Couplers/Grease = 54% of Meta spend" from the meeting notes, it's very likely the MER blowout too.

**Why it's a trap:** a $29 coupler order can't survive first-order math — ~$12 COGS + $30 shipping + any paid CAC = **underwater on order one.** It only pays back if the coupler buyer graduates to packs → covers/mats. But your own cohort data says **only ~5% of grease-first buyers ever cross-sell to non-grease, and it happens in the first basket or never.** So we're paying cold-traffic CAC to acquire cheap consumable buyers who mostly stay cheap. **AOV collapse and MER blowout are the same mechanism.**

## Problem 2: the Hauler WFU sale didn't pull its weight
Discounts ran **$2.4k–$3.1k/day on 13–15 Jul (14–18% of gross)**, but the Hauler bag itself did only **$5,441 / 14 orders** all week. The discount was broader than the Hauler contribution — i.e. it subsidised buyers who'd have paid full price. Margin drag for a thin, specific return.

## Problem 3: conversion stayed weak *during a sale*
CVR sat at **~1.5%** through a discount week. A sale should lift CVR; it barely moved. That confirms the bleed is **structural (fit / trust / price friction)**, not just cold traffic — a discount didn't fix it.

## What this means for the $399 free-ship threshold (goes live tomorrow, 20 Jul)
It's the **right lever at the right time** — it forces AOV up on exactly these sub-$200 grease orders. But two risks:
- On its own it just adds **$30 shipping friction** to the $29 coupler order and may kill that volume without lifting basket size.
- It needs a **path to $399** in the cart, or it converts worse.
**Pair it with a grease-maintenance bundle** (coupler + tubes + wipes + Pro Mat) so buyers *climb* to free shipping instead of hitting a wall. Chris's call flagged this exact bundle.

## The fix — this week, specific
1. **Stop paying cold-traffic CAC for $29 coupler orders.** Reclassify the coupler as a **cross-sell / AOV vehicle**, not a standalone prospecting SKU. Fund coupler acquisition from **retention/email**, not Meta prospecting.
2. **Ship the grease-maintenance bundle** to give every coupler/grease buyer a first-basket path to $399 and to Pro Mat (your #1 cross-sell). Cross-sell is first-basket-or-never — so put it *in the first basket*.
3. **Audit the Hauler/sale discount mechanics** — cap the sitewide bleed; make sale discounts product-scoped.
4. **First-order contribution by SKU** — build the quick model: coupler vs pack vs mat vs enclosure. It will show which entry products are underwater and set real CAC ceilings per SKU.
