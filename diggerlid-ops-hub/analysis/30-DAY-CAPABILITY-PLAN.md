# 30-Day Capability & Media-Buying Plan
*What we need to learn, buy, and fix to hit our aims — window ≈ 18 Jul → 18 Aug 2026*

## The diagnosis (what the numbers say)
July isn't a demand problem — it's an **efficiency and conversion** problem, and it's structural, not bad luck:

- **Media efficiency collapsed post-EOFY.** MER **43%** vs 25% target → July running a **~$35k net loss**, GPAM ~11% vs 26%. We scaled hard into EOFY and **revenue-per-session halved ($17.69 → $8.46) as sessions grew 2.9×** — textbook scaling into cold traffic. The spend didn't stop converting; the *marginal* audience did.
- **Conversion is bleeding.** CVR 2.4% → 1.0% → ~1.5%. "Traffic maintained, tire-kicking behaviour." Survey names the friction: **price 20.8%, shipping 6.7%, trust 5.4%, fit 5.4%.**
- **Mix is dragging the model.** AOV soft (~$183–190 recent vs $250 MTD) as orders skew to **grease/low-AOV**. Grease is 53% of new-customer first orders and retains best (15.1% vs 12.9% avg) — but **only ~5% ever cross-sell to non-grease**, and cross-sell happens **in the first basket (20%) or never**. Non-grease repeat AOV is **$410 vs $244** grease. We're acquiring cheap consumable buyers and mostly not graduating them.
- **We're demand-gen dependent.** **58% of customers first realised they needed us from a DiggerLid ad.** That's the growth engine *and* the vulnerability — when Meta efficiency dips, the whole funnel dips, and we have thin demand-*capture* (Google/search) to catch the awareness we create.
- **We're flying blind on truth.** MER isn't wired to reporting; we're steering on Meta's self-reported ROAS, which takes credit for demand we'd get anyway. No incrementality read.

## 30-day aims
1. **Reset media efficiency:** MER 43% → **≤30% within 2 weeks, ≤25% by Father's Day.**
2. **Stop the conversion bleed:** CVR back toward **2%+** (attack the named frictions: fit, price, trust, shipping).
3. **Fix first-order economics:** every first order at/above break-even — lift AOV via the **$399 free-ship threshold + first-basket bundles** (grease → Pro Mat is the #1 cross-sell).
4. **Land Zip Mat (5 Aug) profitably** and **prep Father's Day (26 Aug)** without repeating EOFY's thin GPAM.

## Media-buying problems to solve
1. **Post-sale efficiency reset** — a spend-cadence playbook for the hangover: how far to pull budgets after a sale, and the re-scale ramp. This recurs every sale; make it a system, not a scramble.
2. **The scale ↔ CVR/RPV trade-off** — we buy on *blended* ROAS, so scaling looks fine until RPV halves. Move to **marginal / incremental** efficiency: what does the *next* dollar return, not the average.
3. **Attribution truth** — Meta ROAS vs blended MER vs true incrementality. We don't know how much Meta is claiming for demand we'd win anyway. Until we do, budgets are guesses.
4. **First-order CAC ceilings by entry product** — grease, cover, mat and enclosure have *different* retention and margins. One blended CAC target is wrong. Set an allowable CAC per entry point funded by its real LTV.
5. **Creative fatigue at scale** — winners (3 Reasons Coupler 5.8×, POV Grease 8.2×) decay when scaled. We lack the **creative velocity** to keep fresh angles feeding the account.
6. **AOV / mix economics** — decompose the MER blowout into *spend inefficiency* vs *mix shift to low-AOV grease*. Different problems, different fixes (bidding vs bundling/thresholds).
7. **Funnel imbalance** — heavy demand-*gen* on Meta, thin demand-*capture*. The 58% who become aware then go quiet or search — are we catching them (Google brand/non-brand, retargeting)?

## Strategies to go deeper on
- **Profit-based / contribution-margin buying** — set targets on contribution-margin ROAS and **nCAC (new-customer CAC)**, not platform ROAS. Bid to profit, not to reported return.
- **Incrementality testing** — geo hold-outs or Meta Conversion Lift to learn the *true* contribution of spend, then reallocate.
- **First-basket AOV strategy** — because cross-sell happens in order #1 or never: bundles (grease-maintenance kit: wipes + Pro Mat + tubes, per Chris's call), cart cross-sell, the $399 threshold, PDP bundle offers.
- **Retention-funded acquisition** — turn the grease flywheel (15.1% repurchase) into a *higher allowable CAC*: if we know the 12-month value of a grease buyer, we can pay more to acquire and still profit.
- **Creative-led growth** — treat creative as the primary lever (it is, in a broad-targeting Meta world): a systematic **angle × format × awareness-stage** testing engine, not one-off hero ads.
- **Full-funnel** — add demand-capture (Google brand + category, YouTube) to catch the awareness the Meta ads generate.

## Books to read (ranked for *this* problem)
1. **Breakthrough Advertising — Eugene Schwartz.** Market *awareness* and *sophistication* stages. Directly explains the 58%-ad-driven-awareness engine and how to match creative angles to where the operator's head is. The single highest-leverage read for the creative engine.
2. **How Brands Grow (Pt 1 & 2) — Byron Sharp / Romaniuk.** Penetration over loyalty, mental & physical availability, reach. This *is* the evidence base for "category leadership" and buying broad — grounds the media strategy in science, not gurus.
3. **Good Strategy / Bad Strategy — Richard Rumelt.** Sharpen the Playing-to-Win diagnosis → guiding policy → coherent action. Kills the scatter of "do everything."
4. **Obviously Awesome — April Dunford.** Positioning. Turns "fitment confidence / operator identity" into a sharp market position that makes ads convert.
5. **Playing to Win — Lafley & Martin.** You're already using it — go a level deeper on Where-to-Play / How-to-Win now that the data is real.
6. **Profit First — Mike Michalowicz** (skim). Margin discipline for the "lean for cashflow" posture; pairs with the EE model.

## Frameworks to use
- **The Ecommerce Equation** (already in use) — go deeper: Traffic × CVR × AOV → revenue, then VCR/FCR/MER → GPAM. It's the shared language; make every campaign map to it.
- **Awareness Stages (Schwartz)** + **Market Sophistication** — the creative brief backbone: which angle for which stage.
- **Marginal ROAS / MER-led budgeting** — scale to the point where marginal ROAS = contribution-margin breakeven, not blended.
- **LTV:CAC + payback by cohort/entry-product** — CAC ceilings that retention actually funds.
- **Incrementality (geo hold-out)** — the truth layer over platform attribution.
- **Jobs-to-be-Done** — "will it fit my machine / will I be looked after" → the fitment system + PDP.
- **ICE / the Projects Board** (already live) — prioritise the experiments below.
- **Creative testing matrix** — angle × format × hook, with a fixed weekly volume and a kill/scale rule.

## Deeper understanding to unlock (the questions worth answering this month)
1. **What is the *marginal* ROAS of Meta right now?** (Not blended.) Where does the next $1k stop paying? This sizes the correct spend level and ends the guesswork behind the 43% MER.
2. **How much of Meta's ROAS is incremental?** A single geo hold-out would tell us if we're over-crediting Meta for demand we'd capture anyway.
3. **First-order profit by entry product.** At 40% COGS + $30 ship + ~43% MER, a $190 grease order is underwater on order one — so what LTV justifies it, per entry product? This gives real CAC ceilings.
4. **How much of the GPAM miss is spend vs mix?** Decompose it — it changes whether the fix is the media buyer or the merchandising/bundle strategy (likely both).
5. **The prospecting saturation curve.** RPV halved at 2.9× sessions — model the shape so we scale *to* efficiency next sale instead of *through* it.
6. **Creative decay rate at scale** — how fast winners fatigue, so we size the creative pipeline to stay ahead.
