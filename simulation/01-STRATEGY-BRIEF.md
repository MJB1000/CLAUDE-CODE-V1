## Deliverable 1 — Landing page for AI-powered analytics feature

### Audience
- Mid-market CFOs (companies $50M-$500M revenue). Financially sophisticated, not technical. They manage finance teams of 5-20 people.
- Pain points: manual reporting cycles eat analyst time, forecasts rely on spreadsheet models that break, and they lack real-time visibility between monthly closes.
- Funnel position: mid-funnel. They know the product category exists and are evaluating solutions. They have likely seen competitor landing pages.
- Skeptical of AI hype. They have been pitched "AI-powered" tools that turned out to be dashboards with a chatbot bolted on.

### Objective
- Primary: drive demo requests from qualified mid-market CFOs.
- Secondary: establish credibility so the page reduces bounce from paid search and LinkedIn ad traffic.

### Key Message
- This AI does the financial analysis your team spends days on — so you can act on the numbers instead of chasing them.

### Tone & Voice
- Confident but not salesy. Precise. Speaks the CFO's language — efficiency, accuracy, speed-to-decision.
- No "revolutionary," "game-changing," "unlock," or "leverage." No breathless AI hype.
- Think: a sharp peer explaining what changed when they adopted this tool. Respectful of the reader's intelligence.

### Channel
- Landing page — standalone URL. Primary traffic from paid search and LinkedIn ads.
- Must work as a self-contained argument. Visitor may have zero prior context.
- Above-the-fold: headline + subhead + CTA.
- Below-the-fold: 3 value props, social proof section, closing CTA.

### Constraints
- Headline: max 12 words
- Subhead: max 25 words
- Each value prop: heading (max 8 words) + body (max 40 words)
- CTA: "Book a Demo" (exact text locked by brand). Appears twice — top and bottom.
- Social proof: 2-3 proof points. Charlie picks the format (logos, quotes, or stats) and justifies the choice in his plan.
- No unsubstantiated claims. Any stat must be marked [SOURCE NEEDED].
- No pricing on this page.
- No "guarantee" language. No ROI promises without disclaimers.
- No competitor names.

### Deliverables
1. Hero section: headline + subhead + CTA
2. Value proposition section: 3 value props (heading + body each)
3. Social proof section
4. Closing CTA block: closing line + CTA repeat

### Flags
- Flag: We do not have approved customer quotes yet. Charlie must not fabricate quotes. Use placeholder structure tagged [PLACEHOLDER -- REQUIRES REAL CUSTOMER DATA].
- Flag: The product name for the feature is "Clarifi Analytics." Spell it exactly. Not Clarify, not ClariFi.
- Flag: Do not build the headline around the product name — it may change. Product name belongs in subhead or body, not headline.
- Flag: CFOs are skeptical of AI claims. Lead with outcomes, not technology.
- Flag: Legal has not approved any specific ROI claims. Quantified benefits must be marked [SOURCE NEEDED].

### Definition of Done
- [ ] All four sections written within word count constraints
- [ ] No unsubstantiated claims (all tagged [SOURCE NEEDED] if unverified)
- [ ] CTA text is exactly "Book a Demo" in both placements
- [ ] No fabricated quotes or logos
- [ ] Product name spelled "Clarifi Analytics" everywhere it appears
- [ ] Tone matches brief: confident, precise, peer-level, no AI hype
- [ ] Dana has reviewed and cleared
