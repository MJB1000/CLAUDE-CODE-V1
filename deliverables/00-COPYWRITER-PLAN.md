# Copywriter Plan — Deliverable 1 (Batch)

## Approach

**Angle:** Practical timing play. Winter is weeks away, not here yet. The hook is "sort it now so you're not dealing with it in the rain." No panic, no fear — just a mate reminding you to get your wipers sorted while there's a sale on.

**Structure across pieces:**
- **Email (1a):** Opens with the timing hook (winter's coming), gives one reason WiperTech beats the auto-store brands (fit + reviews), drops the offer, single CTA. Short, conversational, no fluff. Works for past buyers ("time to replace") and new prospects ("time to try").
- **Landing page (1b):** Headline lands the practical-prep angle. Subhead bridges to the offer. Three value props: Perfect Fit Guarantee, durability (1-year warranty / natural rubber), and ease (2-minute install + same-day shipping). Stats-only social proof section. Two CTAs — top and bottom.
- **Social ads (1c):** Three variants testing distinct angles: (1) practical timing, (2) social proof / volume, (3) offer-first. Each stops the scroll differently.

**Tone execution:** Conversational Aussie-direct. Short sentences. No exclamation-mark energy. Reads like someone texting you a recommendation, not a billboard.

## Decisions Required

None. Brief is complete and unambiguous. All flags resolved.

## Uncertainties

- Landing page CTA wording: brief says "find your wipers" link or product selector. I'll write the CTA as "Find Your Wipers" to imply the fit-finder tool without specifying UX I don't control.
- Email sign-off: no brand voice example for email closings. I'll keep it simple — "The WiperTech Team."
