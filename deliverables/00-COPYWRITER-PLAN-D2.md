## Approach

**Google Ads (2a):** Three ad groups, each tuned to its keyword intent. Headlines split across five themes per group: brand name, offer, key differentiator (fit/quality), social proof, and intent-specific hook. Descriptions carry the fuller value prop since they have 90 chars. Every headline is written to stand alone — no pairs, no sequences. Plain language throughout, no jargon.

- Ad Group 1 (generic winter): leads with winter timing + quality positioning. Differentiates from cheap auto-store blades by emphasising fit guarantee and reviews.
- Ad Group 2 (vehicle-specific): vehicle makes in headlines for keyword relevance and Quality Score. "Fits Your [Make] Exactly" angle — this is WiperTech's edge over generic listings.
- Ad Group 3 (replacement/urgent): speed and ease angle. Same-day shipping, 2-minute install, "fix it now" energy without being fear-based.

**Landing page (2b):** Stripped-back conversion page. Hero with vehicle selector CTA above the fold. Trust signals as scannable stat blocks, not paragraphs. Offer in its own section. Under 150 words. Mirrors ad language closely for Quality Score.
