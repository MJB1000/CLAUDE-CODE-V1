# Research Skill — Market Intelligence for Strategist

Load this skill when Strategist needs to research a client, market, or competitor
before writing a brief.

---

## When to Use

- New client with no client profile yet
- Entering a new market or channel
- Competitor landscape has changed
- Brief needs current data (trends, benchmarks, pricing)

## Tools Available

| Tool | Use for |
|---|---|
| WebSearch | Market trends, competitor campaigns, industry benchmarks |
| WebFetch | Client website analysis, competitor landing pages, industry reports |

## Research Workflow

### 1. Client Research (first campaign only)
- Fetch client website → extract brand voice, product range, pricing, USPs
- Search for client reviews, press, social presence
- Output: populate `clients/[client-name].md`

### 2. Competitor Research
- Search: "[client industry] [channel] campaigns [year]"
- Fetch top 3 competitor landing pages / ads
- Extract: messaging angles, offers, claims, tone
- Output: add to client profile Competitive Context section

### 3. Channel Benchmarks
- Search: "[channel] benchmarks [industry] [year]"
- Extract: open rates, CTR, conversion rates, CPCs
- Output: add to brief Objective section as realistic targets

### 4. Audience Research
- Search: "[audience] buying behavior [industry]"
- Extract: pain points, objections, language, platforms
- Output: add to brief Audience Context section

## Rules

- **Research BEFORE writing the brief, not during.** Don't interrupt the copy cycle.
- **Cite sources.** Add URLs to client profile or brief for verification.
- **Time-box research.** 5-10 minutes max. Get what you need and write the brief.
- **Store findings.** Research goes into the client profile and brief, not just conversation.
  If it's not in a file, it's lost next session.
