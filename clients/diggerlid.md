# Client Profile — DiggerLid

*Australian earthmover protective canopy/enclosure brand. Suburban-tradie authenticity over polished studio.*

---

## Brand Identity
- **Brand name:** DiggerLid
- **Tagline:** *(none yet — placeholder for future positioning)*
- **Brand voice:** Plain-English Australian, warm and conversational. Earnest, slightly off-centre, never glossy. Speaks like a contractor at the pub, not a marketing department.
- **Voice do's:** "We'd love to hear from you." "Get a quote in 60 seconds." Specific, concrete, weekend-renovation language.
- **Voice don'ts:** Corporate buzzwords, exclamation-heavy ad copy, anything that sounds like a polished agency wrote it.

## Audience
- **Primary audience:** Australian owner-operators of compact mini-excavators (Kubota, Yanmar, Takeuchi class). Self-employed landscapers, small civil contractors, weekend tradies running side jobs.
- **Secondary audience:** Hire-fleet operators evaluating canopy retrofits.
- **What they care about:** Operator safety from falling debris, weather protection, resale value of the machine, fitment that doesn't void manufacturer warranty.
- **What they're skeptical of:** Anything that looks too slick, anything that adds weight without obvious benefit, imported products with no local support.
- **How they talk:** First-name basis. Australian English. "Mate", "fair dinkum", "sorted", "no worries". Avoids jargon when plain words work.

## Visual Identity
- **Palette:** Hi-vis yellow `#FFE600`, deep navy `#1A1F2E`, accent orange `#E84D1C`, earth brown `#3E2C1A`, sandstone `#A89F7B`.
- **Typography:** Outfit Bold (display), Tenor Sans (editorial), Bahnschrift (industrial labels).
- **Photography style:** Authentic Australian suburban tradie photography. Sony A7 III at 50mm f/2.8, shallow DoF, gentle film grain. Backyard/jobsite settings — timber paling fence, brick veneer, displaced turf, clay mounds, coiled hose. Captured candidly, slightly off-centre.
- **Lighting:** Soft overcast late-afternoon daylight OR warm golden-hour backlight through dust haze. Naturally muted, no studio key.
- **Mood:** Rugged practicality meets friendly approachability. Weekend-renovation realism.
