## Deliverable 2a, 2b, 2c — Dewdrop Vitamin C Serum Launch Email Sequence (Batch)

Brief date: 2026-04-02
*If this brief is older than 14 days, Sofia must review and reconfirm before Charlie proceeds.*

### Audience
- Women 25-40 who buy premium skincare online
- Ingredient-conscious, research-driven buyers
- Skeptical of hype — respond to education over hard-selling
- Mid-to-bottom funnel: aware of the brand or recently opted in to the list

### Audience Context
- They already know Vitamin C is a proven active ingredient. They do not need convincing that Vitamin C works — they need convincing that *this* Vitamin C serum is worth switching to or adding.
- Competitors (Drunk Elephant, Skinceuticals, Timeless) have established Vitamin C serums. Audience has heard "stable formula," "L-ascorbic acid," and "brightening." Generic claims will be ignored.
- Objections they bring: "Is this just another Vitamin C serum?" "Will it oxidize quickly?" "Is the price justified?"

- Product name: Dewdrop Vitamin C Brightening Serum
- Key ingredients: 15% L-Ascorbic Acid, Hyaluronic Acid, Vitamin E, Ferulic Acid
- Differentiator: Airless pump packaging that prevents oxidation (competitors use droppers)
- Launch offer: 20% off first order with code GLOW20, expires 7 days from launch
- Price point: $48 (regular), $38.40 (with code)
- Brand photography: exists, warm-toned, natural light, minimal backgrounds
- Customer testimonials: [SOURCE NEEDED] — beta tester quotes not yet confirmed by legal
- Clinical data: [SOURCE NEEDED] — 8-week study results pending final review

### Objective
- Email 1 (Day 1): Drive awareness and first clicks to PDP. Primary metric: CTR.
- Email 2 (Day 3): Build credibility and reduce skepticism. Primary metric: return visits / time on page.
- Email 3 (Day 5): Convert fence-sitters with urgency. Primary metric: conversion rate using GLOW20.

### Key Message
Dewdrop solved the biggest problem with Vitamin C serums — oxidation — so you get the full potency of every drop, every time.

## Copywriter Plan
*Charlie adds approach here before writing. Sofia reviews and approves.*

**Approach:** Three-email arc built around the oxidation problem as the narrative thread. Email 1 introduces the problem-solution (Vitamin C is great but oxidizes — Dewdrop fixed that). Email 2 deepens credibility by walking through the formulation and packaging science, using ingredients as proof points. Email 3 closes with a time-bound offer, using the "you've done your research, here's your moment" angle rather than artificial scarcity.

**Decisions required:**
- Confirm the airless pump is the primary differentiator across all three emails, not just Email 1.
- Confirm Email 2 should NOT include a discount CTA (keep it education-focused, save urgency for Email 3).

**Uncertainties:**
- Whether to use "you" or "your skin" framing in subject lines — leaning toward "your skin."
- Email 2 length could push to 300 words for a proper ingredient breakdown.
