# Copywriter Plan — Deliverable 2a, 2b, 2c
*Charlie's approach, documented before writing. Approved by Sofia.*

---

## Narrative Arc

The three emails form a single story: **Problem > Proof > Action.**

| Email | Day | Role in Arc | Emotional Register |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2a — Launch | 1 | "We solved the Vitamin C problem" | Excitement + curiosity |
| 2b — Deep Dive | 3 | "Here's exactly how and why it works" | Trust + intelligence |
| 2c — Urgency | 5 | "You've done your homework — time to act" | Confidence + gentle urgency |

## Shared Elements
- Airless pump as recurring proof point (introduced in 2a, explained in 2b, referenced in 2c)
- "Your skin" framing throughout
- No hard-sell language. Urgency in 2c comes from offer expiry, not manufactured scarcity
- GLOW20 code: prominent in 2a and 2c, soft P.S. mention in 2b

## Email 2a — Launch Announcement
- Subject line: curiosity-driven, not promotional
- Open with the problem: Vitamin C serums lose potency
- Pivot to Dewdrop's solution: airless pump, formulation integrity
- CTA: discovery-oriented ("Meet your new serum"), not purchase-oriented
- Introduce GLOW20 as a launch reward, not a discount

## Email 2b — Social Proof / Ingredients
- Subject line: education angle
- Walk through the four key ingredients with one-sentence explanations
- Explain why airless pump matters (oxidation science in plain language)
- [SOURCE NEEDED] placeholders for clinical data and testimonials
- CTA: "See the full ingredient list" — drives to PDP
- P.S. line with soft GLOW20 reminder

## Email 2c — Urgency
- Subject line: time-based but not desperate
- Brief recap of differentiator (one sentence, not a re-pitch)
- GLOW20 front and center with expiry date
- CTA: direct and clear
- Close with warmth

## Decisions Confirmed by Sofia
- Airless pump is the primary thread across all three emails
- Email 2 body stays educational; GLOW20 in P.S. only
- "Your skin" framing approved
- Email 2 can go to 300 words
