## Learned Patterns

*Accumulated lessons from completed deliverables. Sofia reads at brief-writing time.*

| Pattern | Source | Deliverable |
|---|---|---|
| Audience-aware subject lines: for skeptical audiences, curiosity framing outperforms problem framing. "What if X?" beats "Your X is broken." | Dana Round 1 | 2a |
| [SOURCE NEEDED] blocks must be visually separated from body copy with a publishing gate. Inline placeholders risk accidental publish. | Dana Round 1 | 2b |
| Anti-disclaimer rule: confident brands do not disclaim their own offers. "This is not a pressure tactic" undermines warm urgency. | Dana Round 1 | 2c |
| Cliche detection: "your X's best friend" patterns weaken science-forward tone. Replace with specific, concrete language. | Dana Round 1 | 2b |
| Locked sections in review save re-review time. Round 2 reviewed 6 sections instead of re-reviewing all 19. | Dana Round 2 | 2a-2c |
| Batch deliverables with shared messaging thread (airless pump) stay cohesive when briefed together with a narrative arc. | Charlie plan | 2a-2c |
