# Email Revisions — Round 1 Fixes
*Charlie's changes in response to Dana's Round 1 feedback.*

---

## Must Fix 1 — Email 1 Subject Line (03-EMAIL-1-LAUNCH.md)

**Dana's finding:** Subject line implies the reader's current product is defective. Risks feeling like an attack on their judgment.

**Change:**

Before:
> **Subject line:** The reason your Vitamin C serum stopped working

After:
> **Subject line:** What if your Vitamin C serum could stay potent to the last drop?

**Rationale:** Reframed from "you have a problem" to "imagine this possibility." Curiosity-driven, not accusatory. Still references the oxidation issue without blaming the reader's choices.

---

## Must Fix 2 — Email 2 [SOURCE NEEDED] Placement (04-EMAIL-2-SOCIAL-PROOF.md)

**Dana's finding:** [SOURCE NEEDED] blocks are inline with body copy and could accidentally render if published without replacement.

**Change:**

Before: [SOURCE NEEDED] blocks appeared inline between the airless pump section and the CTA.

After: Moved to a clearly separated section below the sign-off:

```
---
**[DO NOT PUBLISH — PENDING CONTENT]**

The following items require source verification before this email can go live:

1. Clinical study results: "In an 8-week study, participants saw [X]% improvement in
   skin brightness and [X]% reduction in dark spots." — Insert after airless pump
   section when data is finalized.

2. Beta tester testimonial: "[Quote]" — Insert after clinical data when legal clears.

Remove this section and the separator above before publishing.
---
```

**Rationale:** Moved out of the email body entirely. Formatted as an explicit publishing gate that cannot be missed during QA.

---

## Must Fix 3 — Email 3 "Not a Pressure Tactic" (05-EMAIL-3-URGENCY.md)

**Dana's finding:** "This is not a pressure tactic" is defensive and draws attention to the thing it disclaims.

**Change:**

Before:
> This is not a pressure tactic. If now is not the right time, the serum will be here when you are ready. But if you have been thinking about it — this is the best price it will be.

After:
> If now is not the right time, the serum will be here when you are ready. But if you have been thinking about it — this is the best price it will be.

**Rationale:** Removed the disclaiming sentence. Dana is right — the next two sentences already achieve the warm, no-pressure tone without needing to announce it.

---

## Should Fix 1 — Email 2 Ferulic Acid Description (04-EMAIL-2-SOCIAL-PROOF.md)

**Dana's finding:** "Your sunscreen's best friend" is a cliche.

**Change:**

Before:
> **Ferulic Acid** — Stabilizes Vitamin C and boosts its photoprotective effects. Your sunscreen's best friend.

After:
> **Ferulic Acid** — Stabilizes Vitamin C and doubles down on UV defense when paired with your daily SPF.

**Rationale:** Replaced the cliche with Dana's suggested direction. More specific, maintains the conversational register.

---

## Should Fix 2 — Email 1 "Amber Color" (03-EMAIL-1-LAUNCH.md)

**Dana's finding:** "Amber" may confuse readers whose serums are intentionally amber-toned.

**Change:**

Before:
> That amber color in your dropper bottle? That is oxidation.

After:
> That color change in your dropper bottle? That is oxidation.

**Rationale:** "Color change" is unambiguous — it describes a shift, not a specific hue. Clearer for the audience.

---

## Should Fix 3 — Email 3 CTA (05-EMAIL-3-URGENCY.md)

**Dana's finding:** CTA button text is too long with the code included.

**Change:**

Before:
> [Get 20% Off with GLOW20 →]([PDP-LINK])

After:
> [Claim Your Launch Offer →]([PDP-LINK])

**Rationale:** Code is already visible in the body copy directly above. Button text is now shorter and more action-oriented.
