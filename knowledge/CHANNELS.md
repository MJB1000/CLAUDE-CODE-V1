## Landing Page

### Constraints
- Headline: under 10 words
- Above-fold: headline + subhead + CTA visible without scrolling
- Total copy: 100-200 words for high-intent (paid traffic), 300-500 for awareness

## Google Ads (Search)

### Constraints
- Headlines: max 30 chars, 15 per ad group (responsive)
- Descriptions: max 90 chars, 4 per ad group
- No exclamation marks in headlines
- No ALL CAPS, no "click here" / "buy now"

### Patterns
| Pattern | Status | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Headlines must work in any combination — Google mixes randomly | validated | Google Ads policy |
| No unsubstantiated superlatives ("Best," "#1," "Most Trusted") | observed | [campaign] |

### Anti-Patterns
| What Failed | Why | Source |
|---|---|---|
| "Stop Streaking for Good" without product noun | Double meaning when Google strips context | [campaign] |
